PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY

Ph.D. candidate in Electrical Engineering at POSTECH with research experience in UWB indoor localization, decentralized wireless positioning, antenna and RF system design, and microwave-to-sub-THz measurement systems. First-author publications in IEEE Transactions on Antennas and Propagation and IEEE Internet of Things Journal address indoor localization under field-of-view constraints and RSSD-based direction-of-arrival estimation for decentralized tag systems. Additional collaborative work spans antenna-on-display, shared-aperture antennas, antenna-in-package, and far-field measurement systems.
